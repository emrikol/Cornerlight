# Cornerlight GitHub Pages surface

## Strategy

- Mode: Persuade.
- Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/cornerlight-boundary-plate.png`.
- Thesis: Cornerlight’s deliberately narrow boundary is the product advantage—complete installed-app discovery enters on the left; Apple’s native launcher experience leaves on the right.
- Visitor sequence: recognize the index-free promise, understand the three-folder inventory boundary, trust the native handoff, download the latest signed release, follow the short installation route.
- Nonliteral elements: the comp’s native launcher region is a diagram, not a screenshot; no invented release version, live indexing state, app list, or capability may ship.

## Component grammar

- Graphite drafting field with sparse satin-silver rules and one amber signal path.
- One continuous system plate rather than a card grid. Components are defined by alignment, seams, labels, and negative space.
- Corners are small and machined (2–8 px); the download control alone receives the icon’s clipped-corner amber treatment.
- Elevation is nearly flat: a soft outer edge and inner highlight on the hero plate, no stacked shadows.
- Display type is condensed, uppercase where structural; body type is calm and highly legible. Scale descends sharply from promise to annotations.
- Motion is functional: one amber route traverses roots → inventory → native UI on entry. Reduced Motion shows the completed route.

## Composition commitments

- First viewport: 40/60 split. Brand, promise, copy, and primary action align to the left edge; the boundary blueprint spans the right.
- The icon sits above the promise, not in a conventional navigation bar.
- The blueprint is horizontally legible at desktop width and becomes a vertical handoff on mobile without changing its meaning.
- The primary action remains visible without scrolling at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844.
- Later content is short: installation route, product boundary, and repository/legal footer. No feature-card mosaic.
